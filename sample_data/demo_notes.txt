Portfolio Demo — Regional Sales Notes (fictional)

Executive summary
- Revenue is tracked in USD. The leadership team reviews month-over-month changes by region.
- "Widget C" is positioned as the premium SKU; marketing expects higher average revenue per unit.

Definitions
- qty: units sold in the month for that region and product combination.
- revenue_usd: recognized revenue for the line item (not cumulative across months).

Analyst guidance
- When comparing regions, prefer totals within the same month to avoid seasonality confusion.
- If a question asks for a metric not present in the table (for example profit margin), state that the column is not available instead of inferring it.
